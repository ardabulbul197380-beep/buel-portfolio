01 / Brief

The main challenge was clarity. The site needed to feel credible for parents, easy to navigate for students and simple enough for the institution to maintain as information changed.

02 / DirectionCLEAR.

Large typography, controlled whitespace and a restrained institutional palette keep the experience calm rather than crowded.
